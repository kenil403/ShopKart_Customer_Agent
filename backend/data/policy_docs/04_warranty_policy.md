# ShopKart – Warranty Policy
Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Manufacturer Warranty
Electronics sold on ShopKart carry the manufacturer's warranty as stated on the product page. Warranty claims are handled directly by the brand's authorised service centres. ShopKart does not repair products.

## 2. ShopKart Assured Warranty
Products marked "ShopKart Assured" get an additional 6 months of warranty after the manufacturer warranty ends. To claim, contact ShopKart support with the order ID and a description of the fault.

## 3. What Is Not Covered
- Physical damage, liquid damage, or burn marks
- Damage caused by unauthorised repairs
- Normal wear and tear, including battery capacity reduction below 80% after 12 months
- Software issues caused by third-party apps

## 4. Documents Required
The original invoice (downloadable from My Orders) and the product serial number.

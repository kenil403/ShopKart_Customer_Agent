# ShopKart – Shipping Policy
Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Delivery Coverage
We deliver to all serviceable PIN codes in India. Orders placed before 2:00 PM on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM, or on Sundays and public holidays, are dispatched the next business day.

## 2. Delivery Timelines

| Zone | Cities / Regions | Standard Delivery | Express Delivery |
|------|------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Metro | Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata | 2–3 business days | Next business day |
| Tier 2 | Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Nagpur, Indore, Surat, Lucknow | 3–5 business days | 2 business days |
| Rest of India | All other serviceable PIN codes | 5–7 business days | Not available |
| North-East & J&K | All serviceable PIN codes in these regions | 7–10 business days | Not available |

## 3. Shipping Charges

| Order Value | Metro | Tier 2 | Rest of India | North-East & J&K |
|-------------|-------|--------|---------------|------------------|
| Below ₹499 | ₹49 | ₹59 | ₹79 | ₹99 |
| ₹499 – ₹999 | ₹29 | ₹39 | ₹49 | ₹79 |
| ₹1,000 and above | Free | Free | Free | ₹49 |

Express delivery costs an additional ₹99 on top of the standard charge, regardless of order value.

## 4. Failed Delivery
Our courier partner will attempt delivery up to 3 times. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to our warehouse and cancelled. For prepaid orders, a refund is issued minus the shipping charge.

## 5. Order Tracking
A tracking link is sent by SMS and email once the order is dispatched. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show updates.

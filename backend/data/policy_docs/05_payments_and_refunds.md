# ShopKart – Payments & Refunds
Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods
UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, ShopKart Wallet, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

## 2. Cash on Delivery
COD is available for orders up to ₹10,000. A COD handling fee of ₹25 applies to all COD orders. COD is not available for orders to North-East & J&K regions.

## 3. Refund Timelines
Refunds are processed after the returned item passes quality check at our warehouse (usually 2 business days after pickup).

| Original Payment Method | Refund Goes To | Time After Quality Check |
|-------------------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| UPI | Same UPI account | 1–2 business days |
| Credit / Debit Card | Same card | 5–7 business days |
| Net Banking | Same bank account | 3–5 business days |
| ShopKart Wallet | ShopKart Wallet | Instant |
| Cash on Delivery | ShopKart Wallet (default) or bank account on request | Instant (wallet) / 3–5 business days (bank) |

## 4. Failed Payments
If money is deducted but the order is not confirmed, the amount is automatically reversed by the bank within 5–7 business days.

# ShopKart – Account & Privacy
Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Account Verification
For security, ShopKart support will only share order details with the account holder. Support agents must verify the registered email address before discussing any order.

## 2. Changing Delivery Address
The delivery address can be changed only before the order is dispatched. After dispatch, the address cannot be changed.

## 3. Deleting Your Account
Customers can request account deletion from Settings > Privacy. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Orders that are in transit or under return must be completed first.

## 4. Data We Store
We store your name, contact details, addresses and order history. Card details are never stored by ShopKart; they are handled by our payment partner.
